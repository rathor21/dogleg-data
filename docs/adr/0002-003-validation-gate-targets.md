# ADR 0002: Release 003's Tour validation gates target the modern era, and the putting curve is anchored

Date: 2026-09-07 · Status: proposed, pending Sunny's confirmation · Decider: Claude (orchestrator), 2026-09-07

## Context

The original spec (issue #5) set release 003's Tour validation gate against the hole's all-time scoring average, 3.27-3.28 strokes (Anchor 4, SI.com and NBC Sports/Golf Channel, corroborated). A second gate (issue #9) checked the 2019 final round's own shot-outcome shape (52 birdies / 200 pars / 38 bogeys / 14 doubles-or-worse of 304 plays). No single season weighting could satisfy both: 2019's own published scoring average is 3.053, nowhere near the all-time figure the first gate used. The two gates targeted different eras of the same hole.

A follow-up source hunt (Anchor 9, docs/sources/003_Source_Log.md#anchor-9) resolved the mismatch. Every modern year found (2019, 2021-2025) scores below the all-time average, most by two to three tenths of a stroke. The two years backed by two independently fetched sources each (2019 at 3.053, 2024 at 3.198) average 3.126. The all-time figure spans 1934-2025 and is pulled upward by high-scoring years decades before Anchor 7's own Tour proximity data (2016-2023), so comparing a season model built on that proximity data against the all-time average compared two different eras of Augusta 12, not two different theories about the same one.

A second defect compounded the mismatch. `model._green_strokes` priced expected putts as `min(3, max(1, 1.5 + 0.012*ft)) * (1 + three_putt_rate)`, a formula with no published source. It floored expected putts at 1.5 from any distance, including a tap-in, and charged a Tour player about 1.6 putts from 3 feet against a published 96% make rate there. `montecarlo._stochastic_round` turned that floored expectation into a one-putt probability of `2 - E`, capping simulated one-putts near 50% from any distance and suppressing the simulated birdie rate. The committed `VALIDATION_NOTES.md` attributed the resulting birdie deficit to the Tour aim policy; this putting floor caused it. A follow-up hunt (Anchor 8, docs/sources/003_Source_Log.md#anchor-8) found real published Tour make-percentage and three-putt-percentage tables by distance (Broadie-attributed, corroborated across two independently fetched pages) and a real Shot Scope amateur make-percentage table by handicap and distance band, six tiers.

## Decision

**Putting curve.** Replace the invented `1.5 + 0.012*ft` formula with `model.putt_probabilities(tier, dist_ft)` and `tour.tour_putt_probabilities(dist_ft)`, both returning an explicit (p1, p2, p3) one/two/three-plus-putt probability triple built from Anchor 8's tables (linear interpolation between anchor points, flat extrapolation beyond the last one). `_green_strokes`/`_tour_green_strokes` become `p1 + 2*p2 + 3*p3`. `montecarlo.simulate_tour_season` now draws an integer putt count from (p1, p2, p3) instead of stochastic-rounding a floored expectation.

**Season-mean gate.** Retarget from the all-time 3.27-3.28 band to a modern-era band: the mean of `data.HOLE12_MODERN_AVG_BY_YEAR`'s six per-year averages (2019, 2021-2025) plus or minus one sample standard deviation, computed in the test itself rather than hard-coded. Wired as a plain must-pass assertion, no xfail. Also asserts the MC season mean at n=1,000,000 matches the analytic mean within 0.01 stroke, the same fidelity discipline Gate 1 already applies.

**Shape gate, retargeted and per-year.** Rather than compare one fixed season-wide wind frequency against a single year's shot distribution (comparing a season-average assumption against one year's actual weather), calibrate the one disclosed parameter a season model can fit per year -- `tour.WIND_FREQUENCY` -- by bisection (`tour.fit_wind_frequency_for_mean`) so the analytic season mean matches that year's published average within 0.005 stroke, holding the pin rotation at its default. Then simulate the season at the fitted wind frequency and check the four outcome buckets against that year's published shape within 3 percentage points. The mean match is fit by construction; the shape match is not, so agreement there is real evidence.

Applied to the two two-source years (2019, 2024, Anchor 9) as must-pass, no xfail. Applied to the two single-source years (2023, 2025, PGA Tour course-stats only, no independent second source) as `xfail(strict=False)`, used only if the check fails, since single-source corroboration is weaker evidence.

**Result, both fixes applied:** the gates still do not pass at this release's own default parameters. 2019's published mean sits below the model's own wind_frequency=0 season floor -- no wind frequency in [0, 1] can reach it, a structural miss rather than a near one; 2023 and 2025 share this same problem. 2024's mean is reachable by calibration (fitted wind frequency near 0.29), but the resulting par and bogey buckets each miss the 3-point tolerance: the model underpredicts par and overpredicts bogey at that fitted frequency. The season-mean gate also falls outside the modern-era band. Per the ticket's own instruction, these numbers are reported as plain failing assertions rather than tuned to force a pass or hidden behind xfail. See `VALIDATION_NOTES.md` for the exact figures.

## Consequences

- The all-time 3.27-3.28 figure stays in the article and in `data.HOLE12_ALLTIME_AVG_RANGE` as narrative context -- a real, well-corroborated number about the hole's full history -- but it stops being the number this release's Tour model is graded against.
- `tests/test_montecarlo.py::test_tour_gate_season_mean_modern_era`, `test_tour_gate_2019_shape_wind_frequency_calibrated`, and `test_tour_gate_2024_shape_wind_frequency_calibrated` are must-pass. As of this ADR's original pass, all three failed. As of the 2026-09-07 rev 3 addendum below, `test_tour_gate_season_mean_modern_era` passes; the other two still fail. The suite does not pass end to end; this is a disclosed, publication-blocking state pending Sunny's decision, not a silent pass.
- The putting-curve fix is a real accuracy improvement independent of whether the gates pass: it stops flooring every distance at 1.5 putts and stops mispricing short putts, the actual mechanism behind a birdie deficit an earlier pass misdiagnosed.
- Anchor 9's per-year figures (2021, 2022, 2023, 2025) rest on thinner sourcing than 2019/2024. A future hunt that corroborates a second source for any of them would strengthen the modern-era band and could move a single-source year into the must-pass set.
- If Sunny decides the remaining gap should be closed rather than published as a disclosed limitation, the next lever to examine is the model's aim-policy assumption itself: the sensitivity sweeps in `VALIDATION_NOTES.md` show no single MODELED constant moves the season mean enough alone, and a strict expected-score minimizer may be more conservative than how the real Tour field plays a major-championship pressure hole.

## Addendum, 2026-09-07 (rev 3): recovery-leg diagnosis closes the season-mean gate

A follow-up diagnosis (issue #9 rev 3) broke the season's simulated plays down by landing region and asked which region's bogey-or-worse rate carried the excess this ADR's original pass left unexplained. At the default wind frequency (0.35), the missed-green recovery legs (bunker, rough, long trouble) accounted for about 89% of all bogeys-or-worse despite covering under a third of all plays; the green leg (putting, already anchored by Anchor 8) accounted for the rest. Two Tour recovery inputs feeding those legs were anchorless: `TOUR_UP_AND_DOWN_PCT = 0.50`, a single weakly-sourced pro sand-save figure applied to every recovery including plain rough, and `MISSED_UP_AND_DOWN_STROKES = 3.3`, the amateur convention reused unchanged for the Tour tier.

A source hunt (Anchor 10, `docs/sources/003_Source_Log.md#anchor-10`) found a direct-fetch Tour scrambling rate (0.58, SwingU Clubhouse quoting PGA Tour's own stat) covering the general non-sand recovery case, and corroborated the existing sand-save figure (0.50) with a second independent search. It also found Mark Broadie's published strokes-to-hole-out benchmarks (WebSearch synthesis, corroborated across several independent secondary sources, direct fetch of the primary PDF sources unsuccessful), from which a Tour-specific missed-up-and-down cost (2.94 strokes) is derived by disclosed arithmetic (`E = p*2 + (1-p)*X`, paired with the bunker-regime sand-save rate). `tour._tour_recovery_strokes` now uses `data.TOUR["scrambling_pct"]` / `data.TOUR["sand_save_pct"]` in place of the single shared rate, and `data.TOUR["missed_up_and_down_strokes"]` in place of the shared amateur constant. The amateur path (`model.py`, `data.UP_AND_DOWN_PCT`, `data.MISSED_UP_AND_DOWN_STROKES`) is untouched.

**Result:** the analytic season mean moved from 3.2074 (missing the modern-era band's top by 0.0023) to 3.1365, comfortably inside `[3.0586, 3.2051]`. `test_tour_gate_season_mean_modern_era` now **PASSES**. `test_tour_gate_2025_shape_wind_frequency_calibrated_single_source` now **XPASSES** (2025 was a structural miss under the old recovery pricing; it is calibrable and matches shape now). `test_tour_gate_2019_shape_wind_frequency_calibrated` and the 2023 xfail still fail the bracket check: the model's own wind_frequency=0 floor moved from 3.154 to 3.089, narrowing the structural gap to 2019 (3.053) and 2023 (3.058) but not closing it. `test_tour_gate_2024_shape_wind_frequency_calibrated` still fails, now for a different reason than before: the lower baseline pricing needs a much higher fitted wind frequency (0.80) to reach 2024's higher published mean (3.198), well outside `tour.WIND_FREQUENCY_RANGE`'s stated (0.2, 0.5), and the bogey bucket still misses the 3-point tolerance at that fitted frequency (published 17.7%, simulated 23.8%).

This is a real, anchored improvement to the model's most-questioned inputs, and it closes one of ADR 0002's three must-pass gates outright. It is not a complete fix: 2019/2023 remain structurally below the model's calm-air floor, and 2024's own shape gate now fails by a different mechanism (wind-frequency calibration pushed outside its stated sensitivity range rather than a bucket-shape miss alone at a plausible frequency). See `VALIDATION_NOTES.md`'s "Calibration pass" section for the full before/after tables.
