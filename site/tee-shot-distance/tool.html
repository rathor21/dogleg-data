Sources & methodology

Driver and club-ladder distances, fairway-hit %, GIR %, putts-per-GIR, and the published per-tier average par-4 score: Shot Scope, via MyGolfSpy transcriptions. Fairway width (36 yd, so an 18-yd half width) and the broad amateur trouble-rate cross-check: Lou Stagner / Arccos. Tour holeout-by-distance curves: Broadie (2011), ShotLink Table 9, reused from the Fairway vs. Rough post and offset to scratch level. Modeled steps: tier 30 has no published band, so every tier-30 value here is a least-squares line fit across the six published bands evaluated at 30; the hole-length benchmark is the model's own expected score at each tier's average drive, calibrated so the length-mix aggregate matches the published per-tier average par-4 score; and per-tier lateral dispersion is inverted from the published fairway-hit percentages through the 18-yard half fairway width, then smoothed with a least-squares line across tiers.
