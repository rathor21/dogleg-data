Sources & methodology

Ideal bands and presets come from TrackMan 2023 PGA Tour and LPGA Tour averages, TrackMan Combine averages (the average amateur), the TrackMan Optimizer, the TrackMan 2010 Driver Fitting Chart and the PING 2019 Optimal Launch & Spin Chart. Driver launch and spin bands move with your club speed and attack angle.

The delivery model (how path, face, attack angle and dynamic loft set launch and spin) and the flight model (how the ball flies and rolls) are Dogleg Data models calibrated to those tables. Anything marked modeled is model output or a design choice that no published table contains. Read the Method and limits section of the article for how the models work and where they miss.

The range scene is an illustration. Yardage plates on the painted greens are approximate.
