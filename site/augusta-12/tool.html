What is modeled here

Every anchored number traces back to a published source. These do not: they are built assumptions with a stated sensitivity range, carried through this sandbox exactly as the analysis uses them.

    Handicap tiers 15 and 20 sit well outside the published GIR-by-distance anchor band for a 155-yard shot, so their dispersion ovals come from an extrapolation of the same formula the anchored tiers use, not a direct anchor. See analysis/003-augusta-12/data.py for the full anchor-by-anchor breakdown.

    This sandbox recomputes every number in your browser from the exported grids. No server, no tracking.

    Sources & methodology

    Hole yardage, bunker count, and the general green shape: PGA.com, NBC Sports/Golf Channel, and The Fried Egg, cross-checked in the source hunt. Amateur approach proximity and GIR-by-distance: Arccos and Shot Scope, inverted into a per-tier dispersion oval the same way release 002 inverts fairway-hit percentages into lateral spread. Every pin's lateral coordinate, the hazard boundaries, the wind carry penalty and dispersion inflation, and the up-and-down odds by tier are modeled, each with a stated sensitivity range carried into the aim-point search. The model's own aim for each tier, pin, and wind state comes from a bounded optimizer that scores a grid of candidate aim points and reports the minimum, the same numbers published in the analysis.
